## Long-tail tools

- **`activity_search`** works three ways, all newest first:
  - `query="lot released L-261001-002"` and `"keg lost"` go through full-text search (`maludb_core.text_search` over episodes).
  - `subject="Ed Honour", verb="po_approved"` searches the svpor statements and returns 1 episode (PO-00001).
  - When full-text search finds nothing, it falls back to substring matching and the result says so.
- **`activity_sql`**:
  - Answered `GROUP BY action` over `app.activity_log` and `memory.maludb_episode`.
  - Rejected: `app.lots`, `pg_catalog.pg_roles`, an unqualified `pg_class` (caught by the plan check), `"malu$episode_object"` and `DELETE`.

## Errors seen by the model (actionable)

- An unknown person lists the known people.
- A fuzzy label (`L-26100`) lists the candidate records with types and ids.
- An ambiguous action (`approved`) lists the matching actions and suggests `*approved*`.
- An unknown field is rejected by `extra="forbid"`.
- Without a token, with a records-scope token, or with a revoked token, the server returns 401 `invalid_token`.
