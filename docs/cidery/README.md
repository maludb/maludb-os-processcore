# The cidery's documents

These are `maludb-os-cidery`'s `docs/` as they stood at the fork (commit `adf4733`, 2026-10-08): the research, the planning
decisions, the phase plans and progress records, the customer-orders and equipment-scheduling work, the OS adoption and its
proofs, and the build specs. They are the record of what ProcessCore was forked from. Their cross-references point at the
cidery's layout; the two live inputs, `docs/04-mcp-tool-surface.md` and `docs/05-action-manifest.md`, stayed at the top
until the build order rewrites them.
